How StreamVibe works
Whether you're here to watch or to publish — we've made it dead simple.
1
Create a free account
Sign up in seconds with your email or Google account. No credit card needed — your free plan unlocks 500+ titles immediately.
2
Browse & discover
Search by title, filter by genre, or let the homepage surface trending shorts and featured films tailored to what you watch.
3
Hit play
Stream in up to 4K HDR right in your browser — no app download required. Adjust quality, enable subtitles, or go fullscreen any time.
4
Build your watchlist
Bookmark anything with one click and find it later in your Profile. Your history and watchlist sync across all your devices.
1
Open the Dev Console
Head to the Dev Console tab in the top nav. This is your creator and developer hub — analytics, video management, API keys, and more all live here.
2
Upload your video
Go to Upload and drag your file — MP4, MOV, MKV, or WebM up to 50GB. Fill in the title, description, genre, and rating while the file uploads in the background.
3
Publish or save as draft
Hit Publish to go live instantly, or save as a Draft to review first. Processing usually takes a few minutes — you'll see a live status badge when it's ready.
4
Track performance
Monitor views, likes, and revenue in real time from your Dashboard. Use the API to pull analytics into your own tools or integrate StreamVibe into your app.

API Reference
Integrate StreamVibe into your app

StreamVibe API v2

RESTful API with JSON responses. Authenticate using your API key in the Authorization header. Rate limit: 1,000 req/hr on Pro, unlimited on Ultra.

https://api.streamvibe.io/v2
Click an endpoint to expand
GET/videosList all videos
ParameterTypeRequiredDescription
genrestringFilter by genre slug
limitintegerResults per page (max 100)
pageintegerPage number
// Example request
fetch('https://api.streamvibe.io/v2/videos?genre=sci-fi&limit=10', {
  headers: { 'Authorization': 'Bearer YOUR_API_KEY' }
})
GET/videos/{id}Get video details
ParameterTypeRequiredDescription

// Response

  "stream_url": "https://cdn.streamvibe.io/...",
  "views": 1200000
}
POST/videos/uploadUpload a new video
ParameterTypeRequiredDescription
filebinaryrequiredVideo file (multipart/form-data)
titlestringrequiredVideo title
genrestringGenre slug
DEL/videos/{id}Delete a video
// Returns 204 No Content on success
fetch(`https://api.streamvibe.io/v2/videos/${id}`, {
  method: 'DELETE',
  headers: { 'Authorization': 'Bearer YOUR_API_KEY' }
})
